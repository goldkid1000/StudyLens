      Your writing

      Three ways to check: the on-device check runs the trained V16 classifier right here in the tab — word + character-pattern TF-IDF models with learned weights, trained on real human-vs-AI writing (HC3 plus modern GPT-4/ChatGPT samples). It's instant, free, needs no key, and nothing is uploaded; its measured accuracy and honest limits are printed next to every score. The GPTZero scan and Hugging Face scan run other trained models using your own free keys (below). No score from any of them proves authorship — use them to strengthen your own voice.

      Free API keys (optional) — which ones work here

      There is no single key that unlocks every detector — each service issues its own. The two free keys that work in StudyLens are GPTZero and Hugging Face; either one is enough for a real model scan, and both are free. Keys are held in this tab's memory only — they disappear when you close the page — and each is sent only to its own service, only when you run its scan.

      Essay feedback

      Feedback on the writing in the box above — structure, clarity, and grammar patterns, checked against the reviewer you pick. You get concrete suggestions with sentence and paragraph numbers, never a rewritten essay: the words stay yours to fix.

      Reviewer — each one reads the same draft against a different rubric:

      Criteria StudyLens can actually check get matched to the structure, evidence, clarity, and grammar checks below; anything else is listed back to you as a self-check prompt — it won't pretend to judge what it can't measure.

      Batch file scan

      Scan several files at once with the same on-device V16 detector. PDF, DOCX, TXT/MD, and images (read with on-device OCR) are supported — PDFs are also scored page by page where a page has enough text. Files are read right here in this tab; nothing is uploaded. The PDF/DOCX/image readers load from a CDN on first use, like the Screen tab's text reader.

      Same honest limits as the single-text check: a score is a signal to review, never proof of who wrote something. A scanned-image PDF with no text layer will report “no readable text” rather than a made-up score.

      Writing timeline

Nothing is uploaded, and the timeline clears when you close the tab, like everything else in StudyLens. Download it if you want to keep a copy.

      Optional: connect real AI (Ollama — cloud or your PC)

      Connect a real AI model for the Tutor and for chatting about your screen. Ollama Cloud works anywhere with your own ollama.com API key; local Ollama runs on your own PC.       Voice Coach

      Revision coaching for your own writing — vary the rhythm, cut the stock phrases, and put your own voice back in, so a draft sounds like the person who wrote it: you.

      One rule: this coach sharpens writing that's already yours. It is not a way to disguise AI-generated text so it slips past a detector — if a draft didn't come from you, the honest fix is to write it yourself, and this coach plus the Tutor can help you plan it and draft it in your own words.
