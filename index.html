    Your writing

    Three ways to check: the on-device check is instant, free, and needs no key — it scores the whole document the way real detectors do (burstiness / rhythm, voice, AI-favored vocabulary, stock phrases). The GPTZero scan and Hugging Face scan run real trained models using your own free keys (below). No score proves authorship — use them to strengthen your own voice.

    Free API keys (optional) — which ones work here

    There is no single key that unlocks every detector — each service issues its own. The two free keys that work in StudyLens are GPTZero and Hugging Face; either one is enough for a real model scan, and both are free. Keys are held in this tab's memory only — they disappear when you close the page — and each is sent only to its own service, only when you run its scan.

    Screen See

    Share a window or tab on PC, or snap a photo of your notes on iPhone. StudyLens captures it, reads the text on-device, and can turn it into study material.

    PC: Share Screen works in Chrome/Edge. iPhone: iOS doesn't let web pages see other apps, so use the camera button to photograph your notes or screen instead. Captures are processed on this page and never uploaded.
